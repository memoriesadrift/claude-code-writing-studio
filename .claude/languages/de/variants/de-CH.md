# de-CH overlay

Overlay for Swiss Standard German on the `de` pack (spec §4.5). Wins over the
base files wherever they differ; option values in force win over this file.

Swiss Standard German is a standard variety: Helvetisms are correct, never
errors. Do not "correct" them towards German usage. (Swiss German dialect —
*Schweizerdeutsch* — is not Standard German; in fiction dialogue it is voice.)

## Authority

Same official rules, with the Swiss practice the rules allow: *Duden –
Schweizerhochdeutsch* and the *Schweizer Orthographische Konferenz* (SOK)
recommendations; federal texts follow the *Leitfaden* of the Federal
Chancellery.

## Spelling

- **No ß.** Always *ss*: *Strasse*, *gross*, *heissen*, *ausser*, *Fuss*,
  *dass*. Treat any ß in the author's text as an inconsistency (except in
  proper names and quotations from German or Austrian sources, which keep it).
- Capitalised *ẞ* never appears.
- French-origin spellings are common: *Communiqué*, *Portemonnaie*,
  *Billett*, *Perron*, *Trottoir*.

## Punctuation

- **Quotation marks:** guillemets «…» with ‹…› inside, pointing outwards,
  without spaces inside (unlike French): *«Ich komme», sagte sie.*
- Everything else as in the base pack.

## Numbers

- Thousands: apostrophe *1'000'000* (or a narrow space); decimal comma in
  running text (*3,5 %*), but a decimal point in amounts of money:
  *Fr. 3.50*, *CHF 12.–*.

## Grammar

- Perfect with *sein* for *sitzen, stehen, liegen* is common and standard.
- Some genders differ: *das Tram*, *der Match* (sport), *das E-Mail*.

## Dates

- *9. November 2026*; numeric *9.11.2026*.
